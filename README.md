# Paywall Audit — Piknik (Sago Mini World)

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/piknik-paywall-audit/

Four ranked ideas for the Sago Mini World paywall, each backed by a number from Adapty's State of Paywalls 2026, plus a clickable landscape rebuild of the paywall and the offer shown on close, in Piknik's own design. Use the Today / Proposed switch to compare.

| # | Idea | Report anchor | Outcome |
|---|------|---------------|---------|
| 1 | Pre-select Annual and put the trial on it | 3.4: 78% of top trial paywalls pre-select annual · 2.4 · 5.3 annual-forward layout | ARPU +25–40% (winning A/B tests) |
| 2 | Say what happens in the 7 days | 3.4: 74% add reassurance, 24% show a timeline · 1.6 / table E: 40% of US trials cancelled within 24 h · 5.3 trial explainer | ARPU +12–36% (winning A/B tests) |
| 3 | Bring “100 million families” onto the paywall | 3.1: 27% show social proof · 3.4: 20% of trial paywalls show awards · 5.3 | CR +10–30% (winning A/B tests) |
| 4 | Show the close button after 3 seconds | 3.7: 22% of apps delay it, 50% of those by 3–5 s · 5.3 | CR +8–15% (winning A/B tests) |

Kept as is: the 10-minute second-chance offer (4.5: only 4% of apps show one).

**Slice:** Education × US where the report breaks it out, all categories / all regions elsewhere. App Store data, Oct 2025–Sep 2026.

**Report:** https://adapty.io/state-of-paywalls/?utm_source=paywall-audit&utm_medium=github-pages&utm_campaign=sop_2026&utm_content=piknik

**Grounding.** Prices (€9.99/month, €63.99/year, €5.33/month World only, €39.99/year offer), copy and proof (“Trusted by over 100 million families”, “700,000+ five star reviews”) come from Piknik's own in-app screens; Editors' Choice and the Parents' Choice Gold Award come from the App Store listing. Prices are shown as they appear in a euro storefront. The Day 5 reminder in the timeline is part of the proposal, not a current feature.

Impact ranges are observed lifts from winning A/B tests in State of Paywalls 2026 and Adapty's own data, not measured lift for Piknik.

Run locally: `python3 -m http.server` in this folder, then open http://localhost:8000.
