What this is

We opened Sago Mini World as a parent would, then held your paywall up against the market.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, and this year we published what 78,000+ of their paywalls show: what converts, what top apps put on screen, and what wins A/B tests. We installed Sago Mini World, went through the parent gate to your paywall, tapped every plan and closed it to see what comes next. Then we checked each part against the report.

What follows is four ideas, not fifteen. Each is a test you could run, and each is built into a clickable prototype, landscape like your app. The prototype uses your design, not ours. Benchmarks are for Education apps in the US where the report breaks them out, and for all apps where it doesn’t.

We did not redesign your app

Your wavy blue background, your app-icon column, your plan cards with the blue check and green “Save 45%”, your blue pill button and your 10-minute offer all stay. We moved a few things you already have and added three short lines.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If you have already run one of these, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest.

The four ideas

Ranked by what we would test first.

Offer changes come first: in the report, trial and plan changes win more A/B tests than visual or copy changes. Each idea opens on its screen in the prototype.

01

Pre-select Annual and put the trial on it

When the paywall opens, Monthly is ticked, so the default trial leads into €9.99 a month. Annual at €63.99 a year, your “Save 45%” plan, waits unselected below it.

📊State of Paywalls 2026 · 78% of top trial paywalls pre-select annual, and doing so doesn’t hurt conversion in Adapty data.

A/B tests
02

Say what happens in the 7 days

The button says “Try free for 7 days” and that is all a parent learns. Nothing says when they are charged or that they can cancel, even though your App Store listing already promises “cancel anytime”. Add a three-step timeline and one line under the button.

📊State of Paywalls 2026 (US) · 40% of trials are cancelled within 24 hours. 74% of top trial paywalls add reassurance.
See the screen ARPU +12–36%Observed in winning
A/B tests
03

Bring “100 million families” onto the paywall

“Trusted by over 100 million families” and “700,000+ five star reviews” appear on the screen before the paywall, then disappear when the price does. Your App Store Editors’ Choice and Parents’ Choice Gold Award don’t appear anywhere in the flow. The waves on the left have room for all of it.

📊State of Paywalls 2026 · only 27% of top paywalls show social proof; 20% of trial paywalls show awards.
See the screen CR +10–30%Observed in winning
A/B tests
04

Show the close button after 3 seconds

The X is there the moment the paywall opens, before a parent has read the plans. A short delay gives the plans a few seconds first. Your 10-minute offer still waits behind the X.

📊State of Paywalls 2026 · 22% of apps delay the close button; half of those wait 3–5 seconds.

Potential outcome

All four work on one moment: a parent who has just passed your gate and is looking at the price. Three change what the paywall offers and explains, one changes its timing. Your 10-minute offer already puts you among the 4% of apps with a second chance, so it stays as it is.
